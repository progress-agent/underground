The geometry uses an illustrative level floor at `renderM`, with 3:1 interior slopes for reservoirs and vertical dock quays. There is no surveyed bathymetric mesh. The datum is canonical real metres multiplied by VE, so Master stretches landscape, banks and depth together. Where a published water/crest AOD is absent, the median DEM elevation at an outside toe is the surrounding-land datum. Estimated freeboard and crest width are model assumptions, not measured facts. The crest is 4 m wide unless a site-specific fact supplies it.
